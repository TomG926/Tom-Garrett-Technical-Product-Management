Multilingual Contact Centre Voice infrastructure · Dec 2024 – Jun 2026

Product and delivery lead across 7 contributors, working directly with engineering on the data model, the conference and routing services, and the Twilio Voice SDK in the browser. Routing is row-locked so two callers can never be handed the same agent; the supervisor console deliberately surfaces online, available-in-database and available-in-Redis as three separate indicators, because in production those three disagree.

~5,000 calls a month  ·  9 languages in the IVR  ·  28 webhook and agent-action endpoints  ·  47 schema migrations against a live product

AI-Assisted High-Net Wealth Dashboard Personal wealth platform · Oct 2025 – Aug 2026

Two ingestion paths into a 22-table domain model — TrueLayer open banking for accounts and transactions, GPT-4o vision for pension and mortgage statements — feeding a retirement engine built for Irish rules. 0→1 from problem definition to production use by 100+ clients; the closing stretch was a test-suite rebuild that surfaced a bank integration which had never once produced a record.

22 domain tables  ·  24 screens, member and adviser  ·  test-suite rebuild from 31.9% to 91.6% line coverage across 1,097 tests

Self-Updating AI Legal Knowledge Base AI legal intelligence · Jun – Aug 2026

A knowledge base of US deposit law that keeps itself current and never writes to itself. Three Claude agents research the live web, adversarially fact-check each other, then emit schema-valid proposals a human reviewer accepts, edits or declines. The final stage is tool-free and schema-bound: fourteen required fields, enums tied to the application's own vocabularies.

3 agents per run  ·  51 US jurisdictions  ·  106 seeded articles  ·  128K streamed output tokens per stage

Human-Gated AI Move-Out Pipeline Workflow automation prototype · Jul 2026

A four-stage pipeline that works a move-out end to end, then stops — because the next step moves someone's deposit. Model tiers chosen by what kind of mistake each stage can make. Pointed at a real property-management export, it revealed the data model assumed gross figures where the document supplies an already-netted ledger: the finding that justified building it.

4 agents in the pipeline  ·  2 model tiers, by cost of error  ·  ~$0.10 estimated cost per statement
